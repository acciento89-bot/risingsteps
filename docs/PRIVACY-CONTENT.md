# Rising Steps — Privacy & Content Release Answers

## Data / privacy
- No custom account system.
- No collection of real name, email address, phone number, postal address or external account credentials.
- No third-party analytics SDK in the V1 source.
- No advertising SDK in the V1 source.
- Persistence uses Roblox DataStore for gameplay/profile state only.
- Stored profile data: PB/height progression, coins, owned/equipped cosmetics, settings, tutorial/daily/challenge/achievement state, purchase receipt history required for idempotency.
- No sale of personal information.
- No off-platform tracking.
- No location collection.
- No user-generated text or image upload feature.

## Communication / social
- No custom chat system.
- No voice-chat dependency.
- No direct messaging feature implemented by Rising Steps.
- No external links required for core gameplay.

## Content
- Cartoon/stylized platforming.
- Falling/failure only; no blood, gore or realistic injury.
- No weapons.
- No gambling or loot-box mechanic.
- No alcohol, nicotine or drug content.
- No sexual content.
- No horror imagery.
- No political content.
- Monetization consists of explicit products/passes/cosmetics; no randomized paid rewards.

## Monetization disclosure
- Revive: explicit user-triggered purchase path only.
- Temporary Stability assistance: explicit duration and assisted-run semantics.
- Coin Multiplier: affects cosmetic currency only, not competitive height.
- Cosmetics: visual-only.
- No purchase prompt on first spawn.

## Release verification still required in Creator Dashboard
The answers above are the canonical source for the Roblox experience questionnaire. Dashboard submission itself must match these answers exactly.
